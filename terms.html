1. About Aplomb

Aplomb provides two mobile applications:

  • Aplomb Technique — analyses ballet technique from phone video using AI pose estimation and a Sports Therapy-authored rule engine. It provides feedback on turnout, posture, flexibility, balance, and pirouettes.
  • Aplomb Fit — analyses foot shape from photographs and provides pointe shoe fitting recommendations based on individual foot biomechanics.

Both apps are designed to support dancers, parents, and teachers — not to replace professional coaching, physiotherapy, or medical assessment.

They do not constitute medical advice, physiotherapy, or clinical assessment. Do not use Aplomb as a substitute for professional medical, physiotherapy, or sports medicine advice.

If you or a dancer in your care experiences pain, injury, or discomfort, please consult a qualified healthcare professional before continuing dance training.
